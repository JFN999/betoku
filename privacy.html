Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The short version

  • No account needed. You never have to give us your name, email or password to play.
  • Your game stays on your device. Your progress and chip bank are stored locally on your iPhone.
  • Payments go through Apple. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Ads keep Betoku free. Our advertising partner may collect device data to show ads. Personalised ads are only shown if you allow it.
  • We never sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

Betoku is a puzzle game for iPhone, operated by Betoku based in Norway ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data processed through the app and this website.

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@betoku.app.

2. What data we collect

Game data (stored on your device)

Your puzzle progress, difficulty level, chip bank, settings and purchase status are saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data is not sent to us.

Purchases

In-app purchases (such as Remove Ads or chip packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock it in the app, but we never receive your name, payment card or billing details. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising data

Betoku shows ads, including optional rewarded videos and ads between boards. To show these ads, our advertising partner may automatically collect:

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, language and screen size
  • Your IP address and approximate location (country or region, derived from the IP address)
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown or tapped
  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks

Emails to support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use this only to answer you.

This website

betoku.app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address for security purposes. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on this website. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.

3. Advertising and tracking

Our advertising partner is Google AdMob. AdMob may use the data described above to show ads, measure how ads perform, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud. You can read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You're in control. iOS will ask whether Betoku may track your activity. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and you'll see non-personalised ads. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you will also be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you buy Remove Ads, ads between boards are turned off. Optional rewarded videos stay available if you choose to watch them for bonus chips.

4. Why we use your data (legal basis)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which applies in Norway, we process data on these grounds:

  • To provide the game and your purchases — necessary to fulfil our agreement with you.
  • To show personalised ads — based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To show non-personalised ads, prevent fraud and keep the app secure — based on our legitimate interest in offering a free, ad-supported game.
  • To answer support requests — based on our legitimate interest in helping you.

5. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with:

  • Apple, to process purchases and deliver the app
  • Our advertising partner, as described in section 3
  • Authorities, if we are required to by law

Some of these partners may process data outside Norway and the EEA, for example in the United States. When this happens, the transfer is protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

6. How long we keep data

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to help you, and deleted after no more than 24 months. Our advertising partner keeps data according to its own retention policies.

7. Children

Betoku is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Withdraw your consent to personalised ads at any time
  • Complain to a data protection authority. In Norway this is Datatilsynet.

Because your game data is stored only on your device, you can delete all of it at any time by deleting the app. For anything else, email support@betoku.app and we will reply within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app.

10. Contact

Questions, requests or concerns about your privacy? Email us at support@betoku.app.
