Easy to learn. Hard to resist.

If you know Sudoku, you already know half the game. The other half is how much you dare to risk.

1

Find a digit

Every row, column and box holds each number exactly once. Use logic to work out where the next digit goes.

2

Place your bet

Choose how many chips to stake on that digit. Sure about it? Bet big. Just a hunch? Play it safe.

3

Win or lose

A correct digit pays out your stake times the multiplier. A wrong one costs you the chips. Finish the board and bank your winnings.

Speed pays.

Each digit has its own clock, and it restarts every time you place one. The faster you commit, the bigger the payout.
